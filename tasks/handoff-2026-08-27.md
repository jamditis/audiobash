Two final x64 GUI runs produced different shutdown results:

1. Direct pseudo-terminal launch:
   - The x64 app started under Rosetta, opened one window, loaded the tray, registered shortcuts, started the renderer, and started a zsh PTY.
   - Normal quit killed the PTY, but the Electron main process remained for more than one minute.
   - It had no remaining child process.
   - It ignored `SIGTERM` and required an exact `SIGKILL` after evidence capture.
   - Evidence: `.audit/task-7-electron43-final-x64-gui-red-hung.log` and `.audit/task-7-electron43-final-x64-gui-red-hung.sample.txt`.

2. Normal macOS Launch Services launch:
   - The x64 app started with parent process 1 and reported `arch: x64`.
   - Window, tray, shortcuts, renderer, and PTY startup passed.
   - Normal quit exited the app and PTY after about 27 seconds.
   - Evidence: `.audit/task-7-electron43-final-x64-launchservices-gui.log`.

Five x64 Launch Services runs on the arm64 host accepted normal quit and exited after observed intervals of 6, 5, 4, 9, and 53 seconds. The fifth run used the fresh final x64 package, had a 54-second wall-clock interval, and retained explicit no-main-process and no-PTY results. Runs 2 through 5 retain explicit no-PTY results, and runs 4 and 5 retain explicit no-main-process results. Run 1 did not retain the per-run PTY check. The arm64 control retained its no-PTY result and exited in 1 second. Each x64 unified log contains `LSExceptions shared instance invalidated for timeout` before final process exit; the arm64 log does not. The normal path did not reproduce the earlier unbounded result, but it now proves a 4-to-54-second x64 wall-clock range under Rosetta.

Record the observed fact as an x64-only Launch Services shutdown timeout under Rosetta. Do not assign a cause. Retain the unbounded direct pseudo-terminal result as harness-only evidence that did not reproduce through the normal path; one run does not prove that the harness caused it. Do not add `app.exit()` or another forced-exit workaround. Keep native Intel quit timing as a Task 13 release gate.
